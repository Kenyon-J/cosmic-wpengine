## 06-03-2025 - Cache Background Relative Luminance in `ensure_contrast`
**Learning:** `ensure_contrast` originally called `contrast_ratio(text, bg)` inside a loop of up to 16 iterations. Each call to `contrast_ratio` re-calculated the background relative luminance `relative_luminance(bg)`, which accesses standard linear to sRGB lookup curves and performs multiple floating-point operations. Computing `relative_luminance(bg)` exactly once up-front and reusing it (via an inlined helper) completely avoids up to 17 redundant computations per call.
**Action:** When performing iterative color/luminance operations against a fixed background or reference point, compute and cache the reference's relative luminance/properties up-front instead of recalculating them repeatedly in nested/hot loops.
